# Meeting — expert review with Mathilde Rigabert

**Date:** 2025-11-04
**Attendees:** Mathilde Rigabert, Luca Martinet, Noé Kurata

## Summary

First expert review. The guidance was to make the project's work visible and
traceable: every milestone broken into issues with the optional and mandatory
deliverables marked, all research committed to the docs repository, and — the
point that has shaped the documentation since — the *reasoning* behind technical
choices recorded, not only the outcome.

## Agenda

- Set up all milestones and issues, marking optional and mandatory deliverables.
- Commit all our research into the docs repository: benchmarks, interviews, tech

  decision records, not just results.
- Mark optional and mandatory deliverables explicitly in the milestones.
- Detail every deliverable, every week.
- Add a personal angle to the deliverables rather than presenting them plain.
